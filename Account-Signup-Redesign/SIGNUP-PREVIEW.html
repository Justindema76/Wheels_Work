The sign-up workflow

One email = one account. One business = one company.

Step 1 · Email

Everyone starts with their email. Already used? → "You already have an account" → Log in / Reset password. This answers "Do I have an account?" and stops duplicate people.

Step 2 · Who for

Just me · A dealership · Another business. The answer picks the right form.

Just me

Name, phone, address. No company step. Done.

Dealership

Your details → find the dealership by its phone number (or OEM dealer ID). Found → added to it, nothing retyped. Not found → register it once: name, New/Used, OEM brands + dealer IDs, address, optional tax info. You become its main contact.

Other business

Same find-first by business phone number. Found → added. Not found → register once: name, business type, address, optional tax info. No OEM questions.

Step 4 · Done

"Check your email to set your password."

Adding people

They sign up and find the business by its phone number, or the main contact invites them from My Account → Company Users.

Common denominator

Business phone number (proposed; to confirm in the meeting). It doesn't depend on spelling. Dealerships can also match on OEM dealer ID. Name spellings never decide a match.

Support calls

Customers: email check + Forgot password. Staff: Magento admin → Customers → All Customers → search email → Reset Password.
