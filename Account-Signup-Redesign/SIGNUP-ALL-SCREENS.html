The workflow

How a person moves through sign-up.

1 · EmailEveryone starts with their email. Already used → "You already have an account" → Log in / Reset password.
2 · Who it's forJust me · A dealership · Another business.
3 · Just meName, phone, address. No company step.
3 · DealershipFind it by business phone (or OEM dealer ID). Found → added to it. Not found → register once (New/Used, OEM brands + IDs, address, optional tax).
3 · Other businessFind it by business phone. Found → added. Not found → register once (business type, address, optional tax). No OEM questions.
4 · Done"Check your email to set your password."
Adding peopleThey sign up and find the business by its phone, or the main contact invites them (My Account → Company Users).
SupportCustomers: Forgot password. Staff: Magento admin → Customers → search email → Reset Password.
