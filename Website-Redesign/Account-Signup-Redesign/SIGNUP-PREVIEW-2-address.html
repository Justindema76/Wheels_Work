The sign-up workflow

One email = one account. One business = one company.

Step 1 · Email

Everyone starts with their email. Already used? → "You already have an account" → Log in / Reset password. This answers "Do I have an account?" and stops duplicate people.

Step 2 · Who for

Just me · A dealership · Another business. The answer picks the right form.

Just me

Name, phone, address. No company step. Done.

Dealership

Which dealership are you from? Pick the brand first (Ford, Honda… or Used / independent). Then type the address and pick it from the lookup list. Only that brand's dealerships at that address are shown. Pick yours → joined, nothing retyped. Not listed → register it once (brand already ticked): name, New/Used, OEM brands, phone, accounts payable email, optional tax info. You become its account admin.

Other business

Type the address, pick it from the list → businesses at that address → join yours. Not listed → register once: name, business type, phone, accounts payable email, optional tax info.

Accounts payable

Every person linked to a business shares its one accounts payable email. However many people join, invoices always go to the same place.

Step 4 · Done

"Check your email to set your password."

Adding people

Anyone at the business can sign up and join it. They get the Buyer role and the account admin gets an email. Or the admin invites them and picks the role.

Common denominator

The business address, picked from an address lookup (e.g. Canada Post AddressComplete or Google) so it's always written the same way. Matched on street number + postal code. For dealerships, narrowed by brand first. Name spellings never decide a match.

Support calls

Customers: email check + Forgot password. Staff: Magento admin → Customers → All Customers → search email → Reset Password.
