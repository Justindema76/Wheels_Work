The sign-up workflow

One email = one account. One business = one company.

1 · Who it's for

The first screen: A dealership · Another business · Just me.

2 · Find it

Dealership: new or used? New car: brand (dropdown), then search the address (like Google Maps). Used car and business: address only. If it already has a Wheels account we only say so (no names shown): Join the existing account or Start a new account. Just me skips this step.

3 · Email

Only when joining an existing account, or Just me. Joining: Check if I already have an account (→ Log in / Reset my password) or Email administrator for access. Just me: Check, or I'm new, continue. Starting a new account skips this step: the email is entered in About you.

4 · Details

Joining: about you + optional message → Request access. The request goes to the account administrator (by email), who approves and picks the role, or declines. Approved → set-password email; orders invoiced to the account's accounts payable. Starting new: about you + business (name auto-filled, type, brands, phone, accounts payable email) → Create account; you become its administrator. Just me: about you + address.

5 · Done

"Check your email to set your password." The account admin is emailed when someone joins.

Accounts payable

Everyone linked to a business shares its one accounts payable email. However many people join, invoices go to the same place.

Roles

The account administrator approves requests and picks the role (Buyer / Approver / Account admin), can remove people, and can invite people directly.

Common denominator

The address, picked from an address lookup so it's always written the same way (matched on street number + postal code). Dealerships are narrowed by brand first.

Support calls

Customers: email check + Reset my password. Staff: Magento admin → Customers → search email → Reset Password.
